//Game mechanics and sctructures:

HIERARCHY
main terms: 
CELL - quanta of game world it has pos and obj lives in cells (map of cells is used to make relief, resources, zones etc)
OBJ - any interactive uniq object of game (npcs, cities, entities, effects of spells are objects, landmarks)
LANDMARK - any pesrictant object (usually unmowable) such as city, town, place, battlefield - they could be changed, created or destroyed but such an event is rare

GLOBAL MAP GAME (S, Jirnyak):
mount and blade like world simulation
world_gen steps:
- seed large continents and islands in field array
- use noise diffuse generation to generate organic terrain structures
- generate climate map (temperature field)
- generate char resources as maps over world relied
(forests, iron deposits (more in mountains), clay dedposits (more on beaches and deserts), land fertility (for crops)) (enough for now, will add more after all is working)

kingdoms_gen steps:
kingdoms occupy land tiles (as political maps in real world) 128 total kingdoms (but to be universal all factions are here, bandits and cultists too)
kingdom structure (mount and blade like):

landmarks:
cities (accumulate wealth, population, home for npcs/lords, quests) spawn caravans, armies, piligrims, mages
villages (genereted around city usually on rich resource spots) spawn peasants (they gather resources and take them to city markets)
castles/keeps (home of lords, demons etc.) quests givers and locations
bandit, monter dens (add more after all is woring - sancturies with relics and ruins with artifacts, and locations of spell books etc)

difficulty "level" zones:
again char array on top of game world 
around cities difficulty is minimal (0-10)
around paths/roads (5-15)
forests (10-20)
landmarks (15-30)
in difficult zones more powerfull squads are spawned more ambushes risks and more difficult random events

difficulty level grows with game time passed.

game time:
main map time passes in days
e.g. - 1 tile movement is around 1 day of game world (depends of obj speed with fastes is 1 hour (24 hours?))
game tick = 1 hour
obj are aging, for example player and npcs will be 1 year older after 1 year of game time (365 days?)
population of landmarks updates yealy (simple logarithmic sigmoid growth model)
